Summary: A guide for adding or replacing subjects, changing class sections, and requesting eligible enrollment adjustments after initial registration.

Office: Registrar's Office - Window 2 (confirm location)
Processing Time / Deadline: 1–2 Working Days (unverified estimate)

Requirements:

  • Printed current Certificate of Registration (COR)
  • Accomplished ACE Form (2 copies, if required)
  • Correct subject codes, titles, and preferred sections
  • Academic Adviser's approval or signature
  • Department Chairperson's approval or signature
  • Additional supporting documents if requested by the Registrar

Step-by-Step Procedure:

  1. Check the academic calendar and confirm that the adding/changing period is still open.
  2. Review your current COR and list the subjects or sections you want to add or change.
  3. Confirm subject availability, prerequisites, and possible schedule conflicts with your Academic Adviser.
  4. Get the current ACE Form from the Registrar's Office or an authorized campus source.
  5. Fill out the form legibly with your student details and exact subject codes and sections.
  6. Present the form to your Academic Adviser for review and obtain the required signature.
  7. Have the request checked and approved by the Department Chairperson or designated official.
  8. Submit the approved form and required attachments to the Registrar's Office; ask for proof of receipt.
  9. Wait for confirmation that the change has been encoded; do not assume submission means approval.
  10. Recheck and reprint your COR, verify the subjects, units, and schedule, and report any errors promptly.

Summary: Instructions for requesting a replacement student ID when the original is lost, stolen, unreadable, or physically damaged.

Office: Office of Student Services (OSS) - confirm the campus issuing desk
Processing Time / Deadline: 3–5 Working Days (unverified estimate)

Requirements:

  • Notarized Affidavit of Loss if required for a lost ID, or the damaged ID
  • Current Certificate of Registration (COR)
  • Student number and another acceptable proof of identity
  • Accomplished ID replacement request form if required
  • Official Receipt of ID replacement fee (₱150.00 in the group's draft; verify)

Step-by-Step Procedure:

  1. Report the lost or damaged ID to the Office of Student Services and ask whether a temporary pass is available.
  2. Confirm the exact requirements for lost IDs versus damaged IDs before paying for documents.
  3. Prepare your COR, proof of identity, and an affidavit of loss if the campus requires it.
  4. Present the documents at the designated OSS or ID issuing desk for identity verification.
  5. Complete the replacement request form and check the spelling of your personal information.
  6. Obtain an official payment assessment or instructions from authorized staff.
  7. Pay the confirmed fee only through the authorized university payment channel and keep the receipt.
  8. Return to the issuing office, submit the receipt, and complete any required photo capture or verification.
  9. Record the reference number or scheduled release date provided by the office.
  10. Claim the replacement ID, inspect the details, and ask how to deactivate a lost card if applicable.

Summary: A request guide for students and alumni who need a character certification for scholarships, internships, transfers, or employment.

Office: Guidance & Counseling Office (confirm issuing office)
Processing Time / Deadline: Same Day (2 Hours) - unverified estimate

Requirements:

  • Valid Student ID or acceptable alumni identification
  • Latest Certificate of Registration (COR) or proof of graduation, as applicable
  • Student number and purpose of request
  • Request form or logbook entry if required
  • Cashier Official Receipt (₱50.00 in the group's draft; verify)
  • Any additional clearance requested by the issuing office

Step-by-Step Procedure:

  1. Ask the Guidance & Counseling Office whether it issues the certificate for your student or alumni status.
  2. Prepare your ID, COR or graduation record, and the name of the organization requesting the certificate.
  3. Visit the authorized office during service hours and sign the request logbook if required.
  4. Complete the request form accurately, including your program and purpose of request.
  5. Have the requirements checked and obtain an official payment assessment if a fee applies.
  6. Pay at the authorized Cashier's Office and keep the official receipt.
  7. Return the assessed documents and receipt to the issuing office.
  8. Allow the office to verify your records and approve the request in accordance with its policy.
  9. Claim the signed certificate or follow the release instructions given by the office.
  10. Before leaving, check your full name, purpose, issue date, and authorized signatory.

Summary: Troubleshooting and account recovery steps for students unable to access university student systems.

Office: Campus ICT Support / Official PUP Student Support Channel
Processing Time / Deadline: Varies by cause and identity verification

Requirements:

  • Student number and registered email address
  • Valid Student ID for account ownership verification, if requested
  • Exact error message or screenshot with sensitive details hidden
  • A reachable recovery email or communication channel

Step-by-Step Procedure:

  1. Confirm that you are visiting the official university portal rather than an unfamiliar link.
  2. Check your internet connection, browser, and correctly typed student number or username.
  3. Use the portal's official forgot-password or account recovery feature if available.
  4. Check your registered email for a legitimate verification or password reset message.
  5. If unsuccessful, contact Campus ICT Support or PUP's official student support channel.
  6. Describe the problem and provide non-secret account details needed to locate your record.
  7. Complete the office's identity verification before an account is changed.
  8. Follow authorized recovery steps and sign in with the updated credentials.
  9. Set a strong, unique password and never disclose passwords or one-time codes to support staff.
